## Read this first

1. **Players whose projection moved since the Thursday-frozen run are removed from the option lists** (their Thursday price cannot reflect the news that moved them) and appear on a watch list instead. **Every book price here is a Thursday price (stamped 22:10 to 22:42Z on 10-08, about 66 hours old).** Books have almost certainly moved after the Friday and Saturday injury reports and today's inactives. I have no live player-prop prices. So each option gives the **price you need**: play it only if your book still offers the same line at that price or better. The Thursday price is for reference only. If your book's line differs, the option does not apply.
2. **Probabilities are 50/50 blends of the model and the book's no-vig number**, which shrinks the model's known level bias (it runs 4 to 15 points low on counts and yardage). A prop is listed only when four things agree: the model leans a side beyond its usual bias, the player's Weeks 1-4 games point the same way, he is at least 90% likely to play, and enough books priced it. EV is the blended probability against the Thursday price, a reference and not a forecast.
3. **Labels.** Nothing is labeled edge or confident. Rush-yards rows are tagged RULE because rush yards in a blend is the one non-TD market where the model has historically held up; that permits listing it and guarantees nothing.
4. **New since the 06:58Z freeze (refresh B, 16:19Z, `2026_w05_market_20261011T161823Z`):** ESPN's game-day lists show these Outs: PIT RB Dowdle, NYG RB Singletary, TEN RB Singleton and TE Martin-Robinson, MIA RB Kaytron Allen, CIN WR Colbie Young and TE Endries, ARI WR Marvin Harrison Jr., DEN CBs Surtain and Moss, NE CBs Gonzalez, Prunty and Canada. ESPN shows only 5 names per team, so **absence from it does not confirm a player is active.** PHI@JAX started at 13:30Z and is excluded. The 4 PM and SNF inactives are not up yet; re-check them before betting those games.
5. **Where the model likes a prop by a wide margin, suspect news first.** Examples: Hampton (LAC) at a 37.5-yard line and Kaelon Black (SEA) at 15.5 were priced Thursday for situations that may have changed. The wider the gap, the likelier the book knows something the model does not.
6. **Parlay payouts multiply each leg's best Thursday price**, which is more generous than a book pays on same-game parlays (usually a 15 to 30% cut); EV is shown with and without a 20% cut on the profit. Legs must each stand on their own (blend probability 55% or better for props, 15% or better for touchdown legs, a real projected role). Anything past three legs is a lottery ticket.
