# Week 9 + Week 10 retrospective (2026): hindsight diagnostic replay

Run opened 2026-11-18 21:19 UTC. Engine at `9f00a6d` (origin/main; DECISIONS #65 is the latest decision; config unchanged since 2026-11-03, so this is the engine that would have run Week 9 and Week 10).

## 1. What this is and is not
- **No original forecasts exist.** No branch has frozen `2026_w09_*` or `2026_w10_*` runs, and no odds were archived. Nothing below is a grade of an original prediction.
- **What it is:** a hindsight diagnostic replay of the current engine, using only data restricted to each week's first kickoff (`tools/replay/build_vintage.py`, rules in `var/replay_2026_wWW/VINTAGE.json`):
  - W9 cutoff 2026-11-06 01:15Z (latest depth chart kept: 11-05 15:23Z).
  - W10 cutoff 2026-11-13 01:15Z (latest depth chart kept: 11-12 15:57Z).
  - Stats, snaps and play-by-play are cut to before the target week.
  - Injuries are cut to the target week (that week's report is pregame).
- **Data-vintage limits** (each one biases the replay against the engine, never in its favour):
  1. The line baseline and the market-anchored track use nflverse **closing** lines (known at kickoff, not on Wednesday).
  2. nflverse keeps only a sparse roster for past weeks, so the engine's roster IR/release/trade rules and its depth-chart QB fill-in (`qb_fill.adjust_qbs`, live runs only) are inactive. Replay v2 adds a time-correct availability proxy (`tools/replay/availability_proxy.py`: no report row and 0 offensive snaps in the team's last 2 games → Out; QBs exempt; traded players Out for the old team), passed in through the engine's own `APEX_MANUAL_OUT` file. 81 (W9) and 95 (W10) proxy Outs.
  3. Wrong starting QB in 6 team-games:
     - Replay artifact, 5: depth chart at the cutoff was right, engine couldn't add the QB without a live roster. W9 PHI Milton, W10 ARI Beck, BAL Brissett, MIA McCord, MIN Cousins.
     - Real game-day change, 1: W10 NE, Maye QB1 and Full in practice, DeVito played.
     - Receivers on those teams inherit that error.
  4. No weather adjustment: games were outside the NWS forecast window at replay time, so the model-only totals have none.
- **Sample:** 29 games and about 640 player-games. Two weeks do not establish model quality. Every number here is descriptive.

## 5. Signals: durable, needs confirmation, or noise
- **TE vs WR receiving split (needs confirmation, consistent with the season):** TE yards projected at 1.25× actual and WR at 0.91×. This is the DECISIONS #63 watch item (TE +8.4% / +10.8% over 232 games), larger here. TE yards are over-projected while TE and WR receptions are close, so the error is in yards per target or in how deep targets are split between TEs and WRs, not in volume.
- **Team rush yards +9.1 a game while RB and QB player rushing are unbiased:** a definitional or allocation gap. Team rushing yards include QB kneels and scrambles, and backs the engine doesn't project (#62) take carries. Needs a reconciliation before any change.
- **Home margin, shared with the line:** the closing line over-rated home teams by +1.33 in W1–8 (SE 1.10) and +4.21 in W9–10 (SE 2.08). Home teams are 48.6% straight up this season (n=142, non-neutral). Monitor only; calibration is set against results, never against the market (RUNBOOK rule 5).
- **Availability calibration:** Questionable p_active .652 against .609 played (n=46). Fine.
- **QB passing yards:** essentially no skill over naive (66.7 vs 67.3) with intervals slightly too narrow (.43). This is the first live look at `qb_yds_shrink` 0.7 (#65); n=53 can't judge it.
