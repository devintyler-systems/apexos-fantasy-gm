2. **TD confidence is capped at 85 and most of the Top 20 sit at the cap**, so it carries no ranking information. It is a reliability index, not a hit probability.
3. **17 of the Top 20 anytime TDs are RBs and several share a game** (DET@ARI, BAL@ATL, BUF@LA, SF@SEA). They are correlated through game script. None is independent of the others in its game.

   - CHI: Caleb Williams (hamstring, missed Weeks 3-4, DNP Wednesday) is fixed at 98% active by `_qb_fix`; Bagent is not scenario-modeled. Every CHI skill TD carries this.

   - TB: Jalon Daniels has 30 attempts in 2026 (27 in Week 4). His 250 pass yds is conf 58; treat all TB@DAL passing and receiving numbers as low reliability.
   - NO: Shough (hand, Limited) projects 245 pass yds in a 42-point total game, which looks high.
   - SEA: nflverse schedule lists Lock; depth chart has Darnold at QB1, healthy. Depth chart governs.
5. **Books disagree with the model on who plays.** No book lists any prop for CHI Caleb Williams, BAL Lamar Jackson, PHI Saquon Barkley, PHI DeVonta Smith or NYJ Breece Hall (model p_active 0.98, 0.98, 1.0, 1.0, 0.59), while books post Bagent pass props and Huntley first TD. 6. **Availability is a Wednesday-style practice report, no game statuses.** Swift and Monangai (CHI) are both DNP (59.5%) and drawn independently. Chase and Higgins (CIN) DNP 81%. Thursday TB@DAL is the only game that can be re-checked against a Thursday report before it locks; no Thursday report was in nflverse at the 07:29Z fetch.

8. **Model-only totals run high:** 13 of 15 games, +21.5 points over the slate (TB@DAL 51.5 vs 47.5 line). Use the market-anchored totals.
9. **Fair spread / fair ML in the environment files are sim outputs, not market no-vig prices.** No odds file exists.
10. **Manifest note.** The model-track manifest note (frozen, cannot be edited) repeats "Lines = nflverse schedule spread/total only". That sentence applies to the market-anchored track.
